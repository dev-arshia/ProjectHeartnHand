ProjectHeartnHand

Reconnecting families after disaster — one verified report at a time.

What would you like to do?

Reports are reviewed by a human before any family is told a match was found.

Report a Missing Person Report a Found / Rescued Person Check Case Status

How it works

  1. A report is filed — by a family member, a relief camp, a hospital, or a volunteer.
  2. Our system compares it against other reports and flags possible matches.
  3. A human verifier reviews the evidence before confirming anything.
  4. Families can check honest, up-to-date status using their case ID.

ProjectHeartnHand — built for a student hackathon. Not an official emergency service.
